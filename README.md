# Inventory Spirits
Inventory Spirits is a unique take on a "gravestone" style mod focusing on compatibility and configurability!

Initially, the mod was born after being dissatisfied with existing gravestone/corpse mods, I found them ugly or
that they didn't have the config options to make them work they way I wanted.

So I came up with this... well I wouldn't go as far to say "vanillia" style, but, uniquely styled "graves" instead.
They are entities, and will float to a safer space in the event they end up in the void or lava. Options to control
how many of these can exist in the world at once will come soon.

## Current Mod Compatibility
- Curios

Curios will properly be returned to the slot they were in before death, and will store inside spirits.

- Cosmetic Armor Reworked 

Cosmetic armor will properly be returned to the slot they were in before death, and will store inside spirits.

- Sable

Spirits will track to sub-levels if a player dies on one.

Well, they are supposed to anyway. It's a bit buggy at the moment,
and they interact and lerp really weirdly with sub-levels.
I'm completely lost on how to fix it at this point.

- Jade

Jade will display inventory spirit contents, experience as a bar, health, and change its name to the spirit owner.
